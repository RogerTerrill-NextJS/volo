Goal: active administrators can hide inappropriate flight posts through an ordinary-session guarded operation, with atomic immutable audit and existing request/conversation closure. VOLO-216 owns presentation. No hosted writes, migration application or release.

Architecture decision: retain posts, add `moderated_at`, and record one `flight_hidden` audit per flight. Under the common flight lock, cancel pending/accepted requests, cancel open flights, preserve already cancelled/completed status and existing history, then record actor/target/time/reason atomically. A hidden flight cannot be open. Ordinary reads retain owner access but hide it from other members; participant request/conversation history stays governed by existing policies. A bounded active-admin overview may read PRIVATE post data through its own guarded operation, never conversation contents or recipients. No unhide, hard delete, promotion or new lifecycle engine.
